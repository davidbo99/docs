Phone numbers sit in **your** pool, not in your clients' accounts. You assign one to an
organization so it can make and receive calls, and you can take it back.

## Assigning and reclaiming

**Organizations → Phones.** Each number shows where it sits — **Available** if unassigned, or
the organization holding it — plus how many agents are using it and a link to its rates.

**Transfer** moves it to any of three destinations:

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Your own workspace" icon="briefcase">
    Keep it for yourself.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Pool (unassigned)" icon="inbox">
    Hand it back. Only offered if it currently has an owner.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Another organization" icon="building">
    Any active organization except its current one.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Check>
  **The transfer dialog tells you what will break.** Before you confirm, it lists every agent
  that will lose the number, by name and organization. Read it — that list is the whole reason
  to open the dialog.
</Check>

<Warning>
  **There's no second confirmation.** Clicking Transfer executes immediately, even when agents
  are shown as affected. The change takes effect at once.
</Warning>

## You pay for numbers, not your clients

<Check>
  **Monthly and setup fees for phone numbers are always billed to you**, under your own
  workspace — never to the organization holding the number.
</Check>

That's deliberate: numbers are yours. Recovering the cost from your client is part of what you
charge them, outside the platform.

## Rates

Call and message rates for numbers are **platform-managed and pass-through** — you can view
them, but unlike your [billing plans](/en/organizations/billing-plans) you can't mark them up
inside the product.

<Note>
  The rates modal states this directly: *"Rates are managed by the platform and are not editable
  here."*
</Note>

## Two things to watch

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Deleting an organization doesn't release its number" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    The number stays assigned to an organization that no longer appears in your list. Move it
    back to your pool **before** you delete.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A number can only be in one place" icon="arrows-left-right">
    There's no sharing a number between two organizations. If two clients need to be reachable,
    they need a number each.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
