This guide covers the **Coexistence** method, which lets you **keep using your everyday
WhatsApp app** while the number is connected to Dialtu.

<Note>
  If you'd rather have a new, dedicated number with no app on a phone, see
  [Cloud API or Coexistence](/en/channels/whatsapp/onboarding-mode).
</Note>

## Before you start

<Check>The **Facebook account** linked to your WhatsApp Business number.</Check>
<Check>Access to the **WhatsApp app on the phone** holding that number — you'll need it to scan a QR code.</Check>
<Check>Permission to manage WhatsApp inside Dialtu.</Check>

<Tip>
  Have the phone with you from the start. The process pauses halfway to scan a QR code, and the
  Meta window must stay open while you do it.
</Tip>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings" icon="gear">
    In the Dialtu sidebar, scroll to the bottom and click **Settings**.

    <Info>
      **📷 Screenshot needed** — the Settings entry at the bottom of the sidebar, English UI.

      Save to `/images/en/whatsapp/01-settings.png`
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open WhatsApp Business" icon="whatsapp">
    Under **Business settings**, select **WhatsApp Business**.

    <Info>
      **📷 Screenshot needed** — Business settings → WhatsApp Business, English UI.

      Save to `/images/en/whatsapp/02-whatsapp-business.png`
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect WhatsApp" icon="plug">
    On the **Connect your WhatsApp Business account** screen, click **Connect WhatsApp**.

    <Info>
      **📷 Screenshot needed** — the empty state before any account is connected, English UI.

      Save to `/images/en/whatsapp/03-connect-whatsapp.png`
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Coexistence" icon="code-compare">
    In the **Connect a new WABA** window, choose **WhatsApp Business App (Coexistence)** and
    click **Continue**.

    <Info>
      **📷 Screenshot needed** — the onboarding-mode modal with both options, English UI.

      Save to `/images/en/whatsapp/04-coexistence.png`
    </Info>

    <Note>
      If you don't see this window and go straight to Meta, your account is set up for Cloud API
      only. Contact support if you need Coexistence.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign in to Meta" icon="facebook">
    A Meta popup opens. Sign in with the **Facebook account linked to the number** you want to
    connect, then click **Continue**.

    <Warning>
      **Don't close the Meta window** until you're done. If your browser blocks popups, allow
      them for this site and start again.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the portfolio and app" icon="briefcase">
    Choose your **business portfolio** and, under **WhatsApp Business Account**, select
    **Connect a WhatsApp Business app**. Then **Next**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter country and number" icon="phone">
    Enter the **country code** and your **WhatsApp Business number**. Then **Next**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm details and accept the terms" icon="file-signature">
    Check the details are correct, accept the terms and conditions, and continue.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Link from your WhatsApp app" icon="qrcode">
    When the **QR code** appears in the popup, open the WhatsApp app on the phone holding that
    number and scan it from:

    **Settings → Account → Linked devices → Link a business platform**

    <Warning>
      This is the step people get stuck on. The QR is scanned **from inside the WhatsApp app**,
      not with the phone's camera and not from WhatsApp Web.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Continue and finish" icon="circle-check">
    Go back to the popup and click **Continue**, then **Finish**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify the connection in Dialtu" icon="list-check">
    Confirm your number appears under **Connected accounts**.

    <Info>
      **📷 Screenshot needed** — the Connected accounts list with the linked account, English UI.

      Save to `/images/en/whatsapp/11-connected-accounts.png`
    </Info>

    <Note>
      The connection can take **a few minutes** to show up. If it doesn't appear, refresh the
      page before trying again.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>
