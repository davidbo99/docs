Most connection problems come from the Meta window — it was blocked, closed early, or is still
waiting on something. Find the message you're seeing below.

## During the connection

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Your browser blocked the signup window" icon="ban">
    Dialtu opens Meta's signup in a pop-up, and the browser stopped it.

    **Fix:** allow pop-ups for this site, then click **Connect WhatsApp** again. In Chrome the
    blocked-pop-up icon sits at the right-hand end of the address bar.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Signup was cancelled" icon="circle-xmark">
    The Meta window was closed before the steps were finished.

    **Fix:** start again and work through to the end. Nothing is saved from a cancelled attempt,
    so there's nothing to clean up first.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Still waiting for Meta" icon="hourglass">
    Meta hasn't confirmed yet.

    **Keep the Meta window open.** The page updates by itself as soon as the connection comes
    through — you don't need to refresh or click anything.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="We never received a response from Meta" icon="satellite-dish">
    Dialtu stopped hearing from Meta partway through.

    **Fix:** if you finished every step in the Meta window, wait about a minute and reload this
    page before retrying — the connection often lands late. If it's still missing after that,
    start again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Dialtu was updated while this page was open" icon="rotate">
    A new version shipped mid-session, so the page and the server no longer agree.

    **Fix:** reload the page and connect again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="We couldn't complete the connection with Meta" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    The generic failure. Try once more — if it keeps happening, contact support rather than
    retrying repeatedly.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Stuck just after connecting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Linked on Meta but not yet active in Dialtu" icon="link-slash">
    You approved the connection on your phone, but the final authorization never reached Dialtu.
    This is the most common half-finished state.

    **Fix:** the account shows a **Finish connection** button. Click it — no need to start over.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Waiting for WhatsApp Business approval" icon="mobile">
    Coexistence only. Meta is waiting for you to approve the link **on the phone**.

    **Fix:** open **WhatsApp Business** on the phone holding that number and approve the
    request. Don't close the Meta window while you do it.

    If it times out, start the connection again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A number stays on Pending" icon="clock">
    The number reached Dialtu but Meta hasn't finished registering it. A few minutes is normal.

    **Fix:** reload the page after a few minutes. If it hasn't moved to **Connected** after an
    hour, contact support with the number — it usually means registration failed on Meta's side.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## After the number is connected

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't see WhatsApp in the menu" icon="eye-slash">
    A permissions problem, not a connection one. **Only Admins see WhatsApp by default** — none
    of the Member, Sales or Support roles include it.

    **Fix:** ask an administrator to make you an Admin, or to create a custom role that includes
    the WhatsApp permissions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The agent doesn't reply even though the number is connected" icon="robot">
    The connection is fine — something on the agent is stopping it. Check, in order:

    - The agent is **Active**, not paused.
    - Its **routing** covers the current time, or it's set as the number's default agent.
    - The customer isn't excluded by the agent's **audience** tag filters.
    - The account still has **credits**.

    <Note>
      If a number has no default agent, messages outside every agent's scheduled hours are
      **not routed to anyone**.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Coexistence stopped working after a while" icon="mobile-screen">
    Coexistence needs the WhatsApp Business app to stay installed on the phone and be opened at
    least **once every 14 days**. If the phone was wiped, lost, or simply left alone too long,
    the link can drop.

    **Fix:** reconnect. If nobody on your team uses that phone regularly, consider
    [Cloud API](/en/channels/whatsapp/onboarding-mode) instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
