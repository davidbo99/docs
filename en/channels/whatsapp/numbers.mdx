Once an account is connected, its numbers appear under **Settings → WhatsApp Business**. This
is where you check a number's health and edit the business card your customers see in chat.

## The numbers table

Open a connected account to see its **Phone Numbers** table.

| Column | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Number** | The number in international format |
| **Name** | The verified name Meta approved for it |
| **Status** | **Connected**, **Pending** or **Disconnected** |
| **Quality** | Meta's rating: **High**, **Medium**, **Low** or **Unknown** |
| **Actions** | **Edit Profile** |

<Note>
  A number sitting at **Pending** hasn't finished registering with Meta yet. That's normal for a
  few minutes after connecting — see [Troubleshooting](/en/channels/whatsapp/troubleshooting) if
  it stays there.
</Note>

### About the quality rating

Quality is **Meta's** judgement, not Dialtu's. It reflects how recipients have reacted to your
messages — blocks and "report" taps push it down, engagement holds it up.

<Warning>
  **Quality is worth watching.** A number that falls to **Low** can have its messaging limits
  cut by Meta, and repeated problems can get it restricted. If it drops, look at what you're
  sending and to whom before sending more.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Showing **Unknown** usually just means the number is too new for Meta to have rated it.
</Note>

## Editing the business profile

**Edit Profile** on any row opens **Edit Phone Profile** — the details customers see when they
tap your business name in WhatsApp.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **About** | The short line shown in the chat header |
| **Description** | A longer description of the business |
| **Address** | Your physical address |
| **Email** | A contact address |
| **Websites** | Up to two — the second is optional |
| **Category** | The business category, picked from a list |
| **Profile Picture** | Upload an image, or give a public image URL |

**Profile picture:** PNG, JPEG or WebP, **under 5 MB**. You can upload a file directly, or paste
a publicly reachable URL for Meta to fetch.

<Tip>
  **About** is the line most customers actually read — it sits right under your name in the chat
  header. Keep it to what you do and when you reply.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Profile changes go to Meta, so they can take a moment to appear for customers. The fields
  belong to **the number**, not to Dialtu — the same profile shows wherever that number is used.
</Note>

## Adding another number

A WhatsApp Business Account can hold several numbers. **Add Phone Number** on the account starts
the same Meta flow you used to connect the first one.

## Disconnecting

**Disconnect** on an account unlinks it from Dialtu. Where you have more than one account
connected, it only affects the one you chose — the others keep working.

<Warning>
  Disconnecting stops messages flowing to your agents. Existing conversations stay in Dialtu,
  but nothing new arrives on that number until it's connected again.
</Warning>
