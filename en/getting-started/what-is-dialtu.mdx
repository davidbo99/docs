Dialtu is an AI-native platform that automates your outreach and every inbound conversation across **voice, WhatsApp, and SMS** — with a built-in CRM, sales pipeline, and analytics.

Instead of stitching together five separate tools, a single AI handles every channel, qualifies each lead, books meetings, and keeps your pipeline current without anyone updating it by hand.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Link your phone number, WhatsApp, and SMS, and connect your calendar and CRM. No code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let the AI work">
    Dialtu answers every call and message, qualifies each lead, and books meetings — 24/7.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You close">
    Your pipeline fills itself. You step in only when a deal needs a human.
  </Step>
</Steps>
